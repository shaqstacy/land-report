# Land Report: Huntington Hunting Land

These are ranked hunting-land listings within about an hour's drive of Huntington, WV, covering WV, eastern KY and southern OH.

**Live tracker:** https://shaqstacy.github.io/land-report/

## What it looks for
- 20+ acres, asking price ≤ $500,000
- Deer hunting, plus elk where it's legal (KY's out-of-zone permit; WV has no elk season)
- Room for a camper, trailer or small cabin, or a cabin/house already on the land
- Flat, usable ground ranks higher because it saves on excavation

## How listings are scored (out of 100)
| Part | Points | What earns points |
|---|---|---|
| Price | 35 | $/acre compared with what's typical for the county. Under $1,000/ac earns 33–35, and price drops add points |
| Usable land | 25 | Flat benches, ridgetops, fields, reclaimed ground and water score high. All-steep hollows score low |
| Location & access | 20 | Drive time, a deeded road, and nearby public land/WMAs. Landlocked or ATV-only access scores low |
| Camp/cabin | 20 | A road to the site and electric nearby. An existing cabin or house earns the full 20 |

Red flags noted on listings: no mineral rights, strip-mined land, easements, no survey, back taxes, floodplain.
